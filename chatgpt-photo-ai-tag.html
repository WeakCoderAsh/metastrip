Ye tags hote kahan hain?

Photo dekhne me bilkul aam lagti hai — tags aankh se dikhai nahi dete. Ye file ke andar ke khaas hisson me likhe hote hain: C2PA ka manifest, XMP/IPTC metadata, aur PNG ke text chunks (prompt, parameters). TagHatao yehi saare hisse scan karke saaf karta hai — isiliye sirf screenshot lene ya dobara save karne se ye tags nahi jate, sahi tareeke se saaf karna padta hai.

Gemini aur baaki AI photos ka bhi?

Haan. Gemini, Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion — lagbhag har AI tool file me C2PA ya metadata tags chhodta hai. Tareeka sab ke liye ek hi hai: photo dalo, saaf karo, download karo.
